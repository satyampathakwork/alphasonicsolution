Section 04 • Specialized AI Terms

4. Custom AI & Computer Vision Standards

When deploying computer vision models (including the 50 models in our industrial catalog), the following technical realities apply:

  • Validation & Accuracy Benchmarks: All models are benchmarked against agreed acceptance criteria (e.g., mAP@0.5, Precision, Recall, Inference Latency). Performance metrics are validated and documented during staging under realistic lighting and camera angles.
  • Physical World Variance: Computer vision accuracy is intrinsically tied to optical camera quality, lens cleanliness, focal depth, occlusion, and workplace illumination. Alphasonic provides camera hardware placement specifications to guarantee benchmark performance.
  • Data Isolation: Client training datasets, annotation JSONs, and video streams are treated with strict confidentiality and will never be pooled into public models.
