Section 01

1. Overview & Who We Are

Alphasonic Solution ("Alphasonic," "we," "our," or "us") provides bespoke engineering services including custom high-converting web applications, bespoke business software, cloud automations, and physical computer vision & document AI pipelines.

Our principal website is located at https://alphasonicsolution.com. We operate with a strict transparency standard: we do not monetize, sell, or rent your personal, commercial, or operational data to data brokers or advertising exchanges.

Section 02

2. Information We Collect

We collect information only to the extent necessary to deliver engineering consultations, software deployments, and client support:

  • Client Intake & Contact Information: Name, professional email address, business phone/WhatsApp number, company name, website URL, and project specifications provided when you submit consultation forms or interact with our configurator.
  • Project Assets & Architecture Specifications: Brand guidelines, design systems, system credentials, API tokens, and operational workflows shared under mutual NDA for software implementation.
  • Technical Diagnostics: IP addresses, browser user-agent strings, referring URLs, and diagnostic error logs generated during interaction with our public web pages to prevent security intrusions and ensure optimal latency.
Section 03 • Specialized AI Addendum

3. AI & Computer Vision Data Protections

Our Non-Compromise AI Guarantee: Zero Model Cross-Contamination

Client camera feeds, factory floor CCTV streams, proprietary documents, and training images are strictly isolated. We will never pool your operational video feeds or custom fine-tuned weights into public foundation models.

For clients deploying our custom Computer Vision (Pillars 1–4) or Document AI models:

  • RTSP Camera Feeds: Video streams processed on edge hardware (e.g., NVIDIA Jetson, local industrial PCs) run in memory-resident inference loops. Frame buffers are processed and immediately discarded unless the client explicitly configures event-based infraction clip archiving.
  • Training & Fine-Tuning Datasets: Any raw images, bounding box annotations, or OCR ground-truth files supplied by you are stored in isolated, client-specific encrypted object storage buckets with zero public internet ingress.
  • Model IP Ownership: Custom fine-tuned weights, TensorRT engines, and post-processing heuristics engineered for your project belong 100% to you upon milestone completion.
Section 04

4. How We Use Information

We utilize collected information solely for legitimate commercial engineering purposes:

  • To scope, design, engineer, and deploy commissioned software applications and AI models.
  • To conduct live feasibility analyses and ROI estimations per client requests.
  • To deliver ongoing maintenance, server uptime monitoring, and latency optimizations.
  • To fulfill statutory accounting, taxation, and legal compliance obligations.
  • To protect against malicious automated bot attacks, denial-of-service attempts, and security breaches.
Section 05

5. Data Security & Storage Architecture

Alphasonic Solution applies enterprise-grade physical, technical, and architectural safeguards to protect your digital assets:

  • Transit Encryption: All data transmitted to and from our web applications is encrypted using modern TLS 1.3 cryptographic suites with strict HSTS enforcement.
  • Storage Encryption: Project databases, source code repositories, and archived training data are secured using AES-256 bit encryption at rest.
  • Access Controls: Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP) and multi-factor authentication (MFA) are enforced across all internal workstations, cloud consoles, and staging environments.
  • Edge-First Deployment Option: For sensitive industrial facilities, we engineer on-premises, air-gapped camera vision loops where no video stream ever exits your physical local area network.
Section 06

6. Intellectual Property & IP Rights

We operate on a Zero Vendor Lock-In model. Upon final settlement of contracted project milestones:

  • You receive full ownership of the commissioned source code, database schemas, frontend templates, and custom model weights.
  • Alphasonic Solution retains no recurring license fees, royalties, or proprietary holdback over client deliverables.
  • All third-party and open-source libraries incorporated into deliverables are documented with their respective MIT, Apache 2.0, or BSD licenses.
Section 07

7. Third-Party Services & Sub-Processors

To deliver global web infrastructure, we may utilize trusted sub-processors who adhere to strict confidentiality and security frameworks:

  • Cloud Infrastructure: AWS (Amazon Web Services), DigitalOcean, or Cloudflare for hosting, CDN routing, and DDoS mitigation.
  • Communication: WhatsApp Business API and Google Workspace for direct client communication and technical alerts.
  • Payment Processing: Stripe or verified banking gateways for milestone transactions. We do not store full credit card numbers on our servers.
Section 08

8. Data Retention & Permanent Erasure

We retain client project data only for the active lifecycle of the engagement and required statutory warranty periods:

  • Inquiry Data: Consultation submissions that do not progress to an active engagement are pruned from active CRM records within 180 days.
  • Client Source Repositories: Maintained during active development and 90 days post-launch for warranty resolution, after which clients receive final archives and repositories are transitioned or securely wiped upon request.
  • Right to Deletion: You may submit an erasure request at any time to have your contact details and non-essential project records permanently expunged.
Section 09

9. Your Legal Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Indian IT Act)

Depending on your geographic jurisdiction, you hold enforceable rights concerning your data:

  • Right of Access: Request a complete export of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated information.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit the scope of data utilization during dispute investigations.
  • Right to Object: Opt out of any non-essential technical communications at any time.
Section 10

10. Cookies & Local Storage

Our web application uses minimalist, essential local storage tokens to manage tactile sound feedback preferences (`sound_enabled: true/false`) and prevent duplicate modal prompts. We do not utilize invasive cross-site advertising tracking cookies.
